## Color → Map surroundings — 25 October 2026

This refinement runs inside the existing 3D explorer. Following feedback on the initial photo-heavy version, Color → Map uses modeled ground and lit 3D geometry, without a satellite image overlay. ### Data and runtime

- `src/assets/venue/geography/venue-surroundings.json`: compact registered building/rail data, coverage polygon, floor counts, input hash and height provenance. The obsolete photo exclusion polygon was removed.
- `work/venue-surroundings/osm-surroundings.json`: new compact source snapshot, extracted from the existing `tmp/delhi-nearby-osm.json`. Includes the OSM tags and geometry needed to reproduce the result; no new network retrieval.
- `scripts/build-venue-surroundings-context.cjs`: offline builder. Run `node scripts/build-venue-surroundings-context.cjs` from the frontend root. It imports the same registration helpers as `build-venue-map-context.cjs`: lat/lon → z18 pixels → the fitted venue frame. Widths, gauge and heights use the same venue/ground scale.
- `venue-surroundings.ts`: six active geometry/material batches for lit building volumes, architectural details, window panels, ballast/rails, bridge structures, and sleepers. Building volumes and bridge structures cast shadows. Building/rail/geometry helpers each have their own `venue-surroundings-*.ts` concern. Sleepers disappear at 2 km and façade detail at 2.4 km camera distance, with hysteresis; building volumes remain 3D at every distance. Sleepers are capped at 6,000 and windows at 9,000. No per-building materials, new npm dependencies, or added textures.
- `venue-satellite.ts`: the Map photo helper was removed. The original explicit Satellite scenery is retained. Failed replacement data loads retain the historical volumes. Map displays its existing OSM credit.

OSM colours/geometry remain an approximation. OSM completeness, historical imagery dates, building heights without tags, exact façade details, domes, trackside equipment, and exact bridge clearances cannot be matched from these sources. The existing Hall 1 fit discrepancy (~20 m in the reference notes) is deliberately untouched.

Validation is limited to `npx tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.app.json` in PowerShell, as requested. The user will check the rendered result; no visual-runtime verification is claimed.

## Editable source and integration

- `bharat-mandapam-surroundings.blend` contains a protected existing-venue reference collection, a nearby infrastructure collection, and a property planting collection.
- `scripts/build-venue-surroundings.py` rebuilds the Blender file and exports only the added collections to `src/assets/venue/venue-site-detail.glb`.
- **Building textures, 2026-10-23.** Nearby building walls, parapets and roofs carry Poly Haven CC0 image textures (`textures/`: warm plaster panels for the offices, red sandstone for the southwest institution, weathered concrete for flat roofs; diffuse + normal). `box_uv` in the builder writes box-projected UVs in world metres (3 m / 2.5 m / 4 m per tile), so tiling lives in the mesh and needs no `KHR_texture_transform`. Windows, roof plant and every other feature stay flat-colour.
- **OSM-footprint buildings, 2026-10-23.** Every other building within 650 m of the property's bounding box (all sides: across the railway to the north, the east, north of the Supreme Court metro, the Mathura Road frontage) is extruded from the registered OpenStreetMap footprints in `src/assets/venue/geography/venue-map-context.json` (heights from `building:levels`, 8 m default), outside the property and the ten traced footprints, footprints at least 150 m². Walls use `facade_plaster` (`scripts/make-facade-texture.py`: one 3 m tile = one floor with a recessed window over the plaster, so no window beams), the same concrete roof and a parapet. Footprints are offset 0.25 m outward so the Color → Map extrusion of the same OSM way sits inside the walls instead of z-fighting; the footprints also join `mapMask.polygons`. Heights and façades are estimates, not a survey.
- `satellite-reference.json` registers the Esri zoom 18 image (`docs/aerial-reference/bharat-mandapam-satellite-z18-full.jpg` in the parent project) to the venue's X/Z metres. It holds the image pixels and model world positions of the CC, Hall 14, Hall 1 and Hall 6, and the similarity fit (scale, rotation, translation) that `scripts/fit-satellite-reference.cjs` writes back into it. The fit uses the CC, Hall 14 and Hall 6 (residuals under 1 m). Hall 1 is kept as a check: its pixel pick is right, but the model's Hall 1 sits about 20 m from where the other three halls put it, so fitting it would pull the others 4 to 10 m off.
- `road-connections.json` holds every road and walkway centreline, traced on the zoom 18 image in its pixels. `roads` are the public roads (existing ids kept where it is the same road: `southern-frontage` is Mathura Road, `northern-road` is Bhairon Marg east of the railway, `eastern-frontage` is Bhairon Road on the Purana Qila side). `walkways` are the internal service roads (`type: service`, asphalt) and pedestrian walkways (`type: path`, pale sandstone), joined at junctions for route lines. They come from the user's colour-coded aerial (orange = service road, cyan = walkway): registered to the z18 image on the Hall 14, Hall 1 and Hall 6 roofs, thinned to centrelines, each line shifted by up to 12 z18 px onto the paving it follows on the zoom 19 image (`docs/aerial-reference/bharat-mandapam-satellite-z19-venue.jpg`), and short `link-*` lines join dead ends within 20 m of another walkway or 32 m of a road (see `walkwaysSource`). Sixteen outer roads marked `fromGuide` came from the guide's red bands the same way and were kept only where the z18 photo shows a road (see `outerRoadsFromGuide`); the two `metro-corridor-road*` entries follow the metro viaduct and need a site check. Ids are `service-NNN` / `path-NNN`, numbered north to south. The old photo-pixel `roads` in `reference.json` were removed: they came from an AI-generated render and their geometry was wrong.
- The Blender builder first runs the fit script, then `scripts/prepare-venue-roads.cjs`, which converts the traces through the fit, snaps adjoining ends, splits intersections and unions asphalt, footpath, service road and walkway surfaces into `roads-prepared.json`. Walkway paving stops at the road footpaths; a walkway end on a road centreline is recorded as a gate onto that road.
- `scripts/overlay-venue-roads.py` draws the rebuilt `mapMask.roads`, `walkways` and `propertyBoundary` over the zoom 18 image into `docs/aerial-reference/model-roads-vs-satellite-after.png`, and the Color → Map OSM context (`venue-map-context.json`, by road class, hatched where the site mask hides it) with the traced roads over the whole z18 frame into `model-and-osm-roads-vs-satellite.jpg` (`--natural` draws `delhi-context.json` instead, as Natural renders it). Use them to check alignment instead of judging the 3D view.
- **Color → Map context, 2026-10-22.** `delhi-context.json` is in the geographic frame (east +X, south +Z), but the venue model is turned about 91° and scaled 1.056× from it (the satellite fit), so in the 3D view the whole OSM city sat rotated against the venue; its offline clip had also cut the campus footprint out in the wrong place. Natural still draws that file unchanged. `scripts/build-venue-map-context.cjs` writes `src/assets/venue/geography/venue-map-context.json`: the distant features of `delhi-context.json` and the nearby OSM ways (input: the `read-osm-map.py` output of the nearby square, kept at `tmp/delhi-nearby-osm.json`) through lat/lon → z18 pixel → the satellite fit, with road classes (arterial, local, service, path) from the highway tags, bridges flagged, tunnels left out, and everything inside the property clipped away. The viewer loads it on the first Color → Map switch.
- **Road check, 2026-10-22** (`outerRoadsOnOsm` in `road-connections.json`): roads were measured against that registered OSM. Main roads sat 2 to 7 m off their carriageways and were recentred; four ramps and lanes were retraced from their OSM way (`fromOsm`); the Bhairon Marg tail east of the railway now turns north-east under the bridge to Ring Road instead of running straight on; six roads that do not exist (the lake and fort-wall paths, the two metro-corridor roads, a parking slip and a duplicate junction link) were removed; the Ring Road tunnel ramps, the loop ramp, the Hall 1 tunnel ramp, the Bhairon Marg east carriageway and the underpass link were added. Each road has a `class` for the Color → Map shading.
- **Tree cover and venue surfaces, 2026-10-22** (`treeCover`, `surfaces`, `surfacesSource`): trees are planted only inside canopy segmented off the z18 photo within the old render-traced parcels (which also replace those parcels in the site mask); the 18 grid blocks of the render were removed from `reference.json`. Lawn, plaza, parking and the fountain pond inside the property were segmented on z19 (parking traced by hand) and are built as `SITE_SURFACE_*` meshes that the viewer attaches only in Color → Map, where the surroundings also get class colours for asphalt, medians and footpaths (`venue-appearance.ts`).
- `src/assets/venue/venue-site-detail.json` records the affine registration, residuals, original-model hash, feature inventory and uncertainty.
- `venue-site-detail.ts` loads the added layer before navigation and render batching. Only the superseded exterior office and metro placeholders are hidden. A failed asset load retains the previous scene.
- The near map is suppressed only under replacement roads, building footprints, parking and planted parcels after the layer loads. Landscaping blends near the cropped photo edges; roads continue across those edges and taper only at the outer open ends of the modeled network. Distant geographic context and globe navigation remain available.

Run the asset builder from the frontend project directory:

The original venue GLB is never rewritten. The generated report records its SHA-256 before and after asset authoring. Blender objects retain descriptive feature names; the web viewer batches compatible static meshes to reduce draw calls.

The current layer contains 10 nearby building footprints, 26 road sections, 176 internal service road, walkway and link lines, an elevated metro station and track, parking detail, 1,131 nearby trees in 65 photo-traced canopy patches, supplemental property planting (seven trees and 325 bushes), and lawn, plaza, parking and fountain-pond surfaces for Color → Map. Plant counts describe generated assets, not verified real-world inventory. The runtime GLB is approximately 11.83 MB; the editable Blender file is approximately 36.03 MB.

## Accuracy and remaining source needs

This is a visual reconstruction, not a 99%-accurate measured digital twin. The four anchor residuals describe the fit to the existing model, not absolute survey accuracy. The supplied red stroke and image cropping are approximate boundaries, not cadastral evidence.

Nearby building footprints, road centre lines, metro alignment, visible parking rows and planting belts are traced from the image. Heights, window rhythm, concealed faces, kerb dimensions, metro access structures, lamp spacing and individual bushes are inferred. Supplemental property shrubs are constrained to the existing low lawn mesh. They are not a count or survey of actual bushes.

Roads and walkways are traced on the zoom 18 satellite image, not on the supplied aerial: that aerial is an AI-generated render whose roads have the right character but the wrong geometry. Twenty-six road sections (one component, 40 junctions, fourteen open ends that taper; the globe map shader fades at most sixteen) and 176 internal service road, walkway and link lines are modelled. Together they cover 80% of the red, 75% of the orange and 65% of the cyan the colour guide paints (cyan includes open plaza paving, which a line network cannot fill). The walkways form three networks: the main one, the Hall 2-5 rear service yard and the road east of Hall 6, which both reach it through the outer roads. Eight walkway ends are gates onto a road and twenty end at a building, yard or parking aisle. Widths and medians are measured off the image (about 0.55 world metres per pixel). Mathura Road and Bhairon Road were then recentred on the zoom 19 photo's paving, not on the colour guide, which sits 2 to 7 m off the photo along them (see `mainRoadsRecentred`). Junctions share asphalt, with openings in medians and lane markings kept away from junction mouths. Connectivity is authoring topology, not verification of real-world driving permissions, bridge levels or traffic routing; the flyovers and the Mathura Road underpass are not grade-separated.

Nearby building footprints, outside planting parcels and the parking rows are still traced from the supplied render and have not been re-checked against the satellite image.

Completing a measured second phase requires an original high-resolution orthophoto/site survey, elevation data, and close views of the property landscaping, kerbs, signs and street furniture. A higher-resolution satellite reference can refine the outer road alignments and grade separation. Those sources would replace the explicitly estimated feature records without moving the protected halls.

Station identity was cross-checked against [ITPO visitor access information](https://www.itpo.gov.in/visitors/how-to-reach) and [DMRC's Supreme Court station information](https://backend.delhimetrorail.com/documents/5240/updatedsupcourt.pdf). These sources establish context; they do not supply the modeled dimensions. Existing OSM and satellite credits remain in `src/assets/venue/geography/ATTRIBUTION.md`.

No test cases are part of this authoring script or this requested change.
