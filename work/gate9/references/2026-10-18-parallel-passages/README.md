# Office-side parallel passages

The marked aerial selects only the long corridor beside the office. The three
photographs show the same place from different directions; image-left is not a
fixed site direction. From the office toward the existing boundary, the sequence
is an open passage, a long planted strip, a white barrel-roof shelter with white
supports and low side walls, then a second open passage and boundary planting.
The ends remain open for circulation.
